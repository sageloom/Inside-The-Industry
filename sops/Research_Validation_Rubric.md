# Inside the Industry — Research Validation Rubric & Quality Gate

Before any student-submitted company research is synthesized into an external LinkedIn post or added to Sageloom's case-study archive, it must pass this 5-point validation review conducted by the **UX / Curriculum Lead** and approved by the **Board**.

---

## The 5-Point Validation Checklist

| Gate | Evaluation Criteria | Passing Standard | Red Flags (Auto-Fail) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Verifiable Public Sourcing** | Are all factual claims backed by reliable, publicly verifiable sources? | Minimum 3 credible citations (peer-reviewed papers, patents, official documentation, SEC/regulatory filings). | Sourced exclusively from marketing blogs, uncited press releases, or AI-generated hallucinated URLs. |
| **2. Scientific & Biological Accuracy** | Does the research accurately describe the underlying biological mechanism? | Correct usage of genomic, molecular, or clinical terminology; accurately explains why the problem is biologically hard. | Conflating DNA sequencing with RNA expression, misrepresenting disease targets, or superficial analogies. |
| **3. Pipeline & Algorithmic Concreteness** | Is the computational workflow broken down into concrete processing steps? | Distinctly identifies inputs (e.g. raw FASTQ), algorithms/tools (e.g. BWA, GATK), and outputs (e.g. annotated VCF, clinical report). | Vague buzzwords like *"uses revolutionary AI to solve cancer"* without naming tools, model classes, or workflows. |
| **4. Educational & Career Grounding** | Does the analysis translate company operations into actionable student skills? | Explicitly maps company challenges to required programming languages, bioinformatics packages, and entry-level career roles. | Generic lists like *"must know Python and biology"* without specifying libraries, workflows, or role distinctions. |
| **5. Zero-Hype & Non-Promotional Tone** | Does the analysis remain an objective educational study rather than a corporate ad? | Neutral, analytical tone that discusses limitations, technical trade-offs, and data challenges objectively. | Promotional praise, superlative adjectives ("world-leading", "game-changing"), or endorsing the company's stock/brand. |

---

## Review & Approval Protocol

```
[Student Intake Submission]
          ↓
1. Automated / Agent Sourcing Verification (Growth Lead checks URL validity & claims)
          ↓
2. Curriculum & Technical Review (UX/Curriculum Lead evaluates pedagogical depth)
          ↓
3. Status: PASS or REVISION REQUIRED
   - If REVISION: Detailed feedback returned to student cohort.
   - If PASS: Agent generates the LinkedIn draft and prepares the Paperclip confirmation card.
          ↓
4. Board Approval (Human Board reviews final LinkedIn post draft)
```
