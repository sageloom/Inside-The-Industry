## Minimum Standard Before Publication

- Important factual claims have credible public sources.
- No confidential or proprietary information is implied.
- Inferences are clearly identified.
- The analysis explains connections between different parts of the company.
- The content is educational rather than promotional.
- The final output provides at least one meaningful industry or career insight.
